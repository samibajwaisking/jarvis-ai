# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Jarvis AI to be a friendly, welcoming and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, background, gender, experience level, nationality, religion or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Welcome beginners and answer questions politely.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behaviour.

Report problems to **samibajwa491@gmail.com**. All reports are handled privately and fairly.
